Task 4 - Shortest Path (Dijkstra Algorithm)

Cities and Distances:

Glasgow — Stirling = 50
Glasgow — Edinburgh = 70
Stirling — Perth = 40
Stirling — Edinburgh = 50
Perth — Edinburgh = 100
Perth — Dundee = 60

Find shortest path from Edinburgh to Dundee.

Possible Paths:

1. Edinburgh → Perth → Dundee
Distance = 160

2. Edinburgh → Stirling → Perth → Dundee
Distance = 150

3. Edinburgh → Glasgow → Stirling → Perth → Dundee
Distance = 220

Shortest Path:
Edinburgh → Stirling → Perth → Dundee

Total Distance:
150
